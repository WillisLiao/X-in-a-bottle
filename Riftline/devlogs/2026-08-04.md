## Guard Runner Capacity

`tools/run_godot_serial.sh` now shares a four-permit pool across concurrent invocations instead of relying on callers to limit their batches manually.

`GODOT_MAX_CONCURRENT` defaults to `4`, while `GODOT_SLOT_WAIT_SECONDS` bounds queued work and rejects invalid non-positive configuration values.

The permits remain occupied until supervised Godot children and their descendants have completed cleanup.

Five simultaneous temporary supervised children produced exactly four active permits, the fifth waited, and all permits released after completion.
